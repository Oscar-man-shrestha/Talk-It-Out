# Competitor pricing and paywalled features (consumer community apps and workplace tools), 2025–2026

**Date checked:** 2026-11-26. **Regions:** India (INR) and US (USD).
**How the App Store prices were collected:** On 2026-11-26 I read the "In-App Purchases" list on each app's Apple App Store page for the India storefront (`apps.apple.com/in/app/id…`) and the US storefront (`apps.apple.com/us/app/id…`). Apple shows only about 10 top in-app items per app, and the labels often don't give the billing period. Store prices usually include Apple's cut and can be higher than web or bot prices: Telegram's FAQ says purchases through its bot are discounted because they avoid Apple and Google fees, and X's iOS prices are higher than its web prices. Rows marked "iOS" are store prices. Web prices come from press or official pages, with dates.

---

## 1. Consumer and community apps: India vs US price and paywalled features

### Takeaway
Consumer community apps charge about **₹99–₹900 a month in India** and **$3–$12 a month in the US** for their main personal tier. India prices are usually 25–70% below the converted US price, but not always: Discord charges close to the US price in India. Paid features fall into the same few groups everywhere: ad removal, cosmetics and status badges, higher limits (uploads, folders, storage, history), AI tools, and visibility or insight features. A separate higher-priced business or verification tier (LinkedIn Business, Meta Verified, X Premium+, Snapchat Platinum) runs at about 3–10x the personal tier.

### Inferences
- India-to-US ratios on iOS (INR at roughly ₹85–88 per $1): Snapchat+ ₹99 vs $4.99 (about 23% of the US price); Telegram ₹319 vs $4.99 (about 73%); X Basic ₹170 vs $4 (about 50%); Reddit Premium ₹599 vs $6.99 (about 100%); Discord Nitro ₹889 vs $9.99 (about 100%). Apps that set real India prices (Snap, Telegram, X) cut 25–75%. Discord and Reddit charge close to parity.
- Mass-market messaging and social apps (Snapchat+, Telegram, X Basic) cluster at about ₹99–₹320 a month in India. Professional or identity products (LinkedIn, Meta Verified) sit at ₹639–₹1,900 a month.
- Nearly every consumer tier is built around personal status and convenience (badges, icons, ad removal, bigger uploads). Group-level paid perks show up only in Discord Server Boosts, where members fund their community, and in Blind's paid chat groups.
- Products closest to "workplace anonymity" (Blind, Fishbowl, Glassdoor) keep posting and anonymity free. Blind charges for convenience (ad-free, extra DMs, premium chats). Fishbowl and Glassdoor charge consumers nothing.

### Gaps
- Telegram's current INR price through @PremiumBot or the web (the ₹179 figure is from 2022; iOS shows ₹319). Could not verify without an Indian phone number.
- Discord Nitro US iOS prices: the App Store US page failed to load. US web prices come from discord.com.
- LinkedIn Premium Business INR price, and the billing periods behind the five unlabelled LinkedIn Premium Career iOS prices.
- Snapchat Platinum INR price (not in the India iOS top-10 list).
- Quora+ India price. No IAP on iOS, and no official India web price found.
- Reddit Premium official web price. Only iOS and a third-party figure were found.
- Google Play India prices were not checked separately and may differ from iOS.
- Blind Clean Feed subscription price outside Korea.
- Glassdoor and Fishbowl employer-side monetization was not researched.

## 2. Team and workplace tools: per-seat price, minimums and what is free

### Takeaway
Workplace tools fall into two models. **Collaboration tools** (Slack, Teams Essentials, Polly, Slido, Mentimeter) are self-serve, priced per seat or per creator at about **$4–$15 per user per month** (Slack India: about ₹245–₹557 per user per month on annual billing), and have real free tiers limited by history, participants or features. **Engagement, performance and anonymous-reporting platforms** (Culture Amp, Lattice, 15Five, Workleap/Officevibe, AllVoices, Spot, Vault) are sales-led, billed annually, often with **minimum annual spend of about $4,000–$5,000**, and offer no free tier. AI and analytics sit in the higher tiers on both sides.

Vendr's median buyer pays **$15,750 per year** (range $7,421–$38,985, 33 purchases) ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/allvoices)); Software Advice lists "from $8/mo" ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/hr/allvoices-profile)) | Starter includes up to 2 admin seats ([AllVoices](https://allvoices.co/pricing)) | Anonymous reporting is the core product. Price grows with admin or investigator seats and add-ons such as language support |
| **Spot (anonymous HR reporting chatbot)** | None found | Pro $249/mo ($2,490/yr); Unlimited $499/mo; Enterprise from $999/mo ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/hr/spot-profile/)). In 2018: $800/yr flat for up to 100 people ([TechCrunch 2018](https://techcrunch.com/2018/10/23/spot-launches-chatbot-for-hr-departments-to-address-harassment-and-discrimination)) | Flat-rate tiers | Not verified on Spot's own site |
| **Vault Platform (speak-up / misconduct reporting)** | No free version or trial | Quote-based; mid-market package priced on headcount ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/vault-platform-vault-platform/pricing); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/205094/Vault-Platform/)) | n/a | n/a |

### Inferences
- Collaboration tools gate on **history or retention** (Slack 90 days, Polly 45 days), **participant counts** (Slido 100, Mentimeter 50 a month, Teams 100 people and 60 minutes), and **admin or brand controls** (moderation, branding, SSO). Personal cosmetics play no role.
- Engagement and HR-feedback platforms price per employee ($4–$16 PEPM), but their real floor is the annual minimum ($4,000 at Lattice, about $4,500 at Culture Amp, $4,999 base at Workleap). That puts them out of reach for a 10–30 person startup, agency or college club.
- Anonymous reporting and hotline tools (AllVoices, Spot, Vault) are the most expensive category: about $2,500–$16,000+ a year, quote-based, and priced on headcount plus admin or investigator seats. They sell anonymity, compliance and case management to HR buyers, not to employees.
- Workleap's 2025–2026 model ($4,999 base plus $32 per manager) charges per **manager or controller**, not per employee. This is a known pattern for products where only the people running the system get value from the paid features.

### Gaps
- Slido's official feature-by-tier breakdown and India prices; the official page didn't render.
- India (INR) prices for Mentimeter, Polly, 15Five, Lattice, Culture Amp, Workleap, AllVoices, Spot and Vault. None were found; all are quoted in USD.
- Slack India: the terms and duration of the struck-through annual discount (₹545 → ₹245.25).
- Officevibe's current standalone price and whether a free plan still exists; the current Workleap page shows no free plan.
- Microsoft Teams Essentials: whether the 50-seat direct-purchase limit still applies, and India's monthly billing option.
- Culture Amp and AllVoices: no official list prices; only third-party or procurement estimates.

## 3. Which paid features are most common and which are reported to drive conversion

### Takeaway
The most common paid features are **ad removal, higher limits (uploads, history, storage, participants), cosmetics and status badges, and AI tools**. The strongest *reported* conversion and revenue drivers in 2025–2026 are **AI features** (LinkedIn, Snap Lens+, Slack's 2025 repackaging) and **history or storage limits** (Slack's 90-day limit, Snapchat's Memories cap). Benchmark data shows hard paywalls convert about 5x better than freemium, and India and Southeast Asia have the lowest per-payer value of any region.

### Cited Findings

**How often each paid feature appears among the products in sections 1–2**
- **Ad-free:** Telegram (no sponsored messages), Reddit Premium, X Premium+, Quora+, Snapchat Platinum, Blind Clean Feed ([Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq_premium); [ContextBolt](https://contextbolt.com/blog/reddit-premium-vs-free/); [Tech.co](https://tech.co/news/x-premium-price-increase-grok-3); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2186130); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/snapchat-caps-free-memory-storage-launches-paid-storage-plans); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534965)).
- **Cosmetics and status (badges, icons, profiles, emoji):** Telegram (badge, icons, emoji status), Discord (badge, profiles, emoji, themes), Reddit (avatar gear, icons), X (checkmark) ([Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq_premium); [discord.com/nitro](https://discord.com/nitro); [Tech.co](https://tech.co/news/x-premium-price-increase-grok-3)).
- **Higher limits:** Telegram (4 GB uploads, doubled folder and pin limits), Discord (50 MB / 1 GB uploads, 4,000-character messages, 200 servers), X Basic (25,000-character posts, 3-hour videos), Slack (history, apps), Polly (history), Slido and Mentimeter (participants), Snapchat (Memories storage) ([sources as above](https://telegram.org/faq_premium); [discord.com/nitro](https://discord.com/nitro); [Slack India](https://slack.com/intl/en-in/pricing); [Polly](https://www.polly.ai/pricing); [Mentimeter](https://www.mentimeter.com/plans)).
- **AI:** X (Grok), LinkedIn (AI writing and job insights), Telegram (transcription, translation), Snap Lens+ (generative Lenses), Slack (summaries, recaps, AI search), 15Five, Lattice and Workleap (AI analysis of survey feedback) ([Tech.co](https://tech.co/news/x-premium-price-increase-grok-3); [LinkedIn Premium](https://premium.linkedin.com/careers/career); [Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq_premium); [Snap Newsroom](https://newsroom.snap.com/snapchat-subscription-offerings-surpass-30-million); [Slack India](https://slack.com/intl/en-in/pricing); [15Five](https://www.15five.com/pricing); [Lattice](https://lattice.com/pricing); [Workleap](https://workleap.com/pricing)).

**Reported conversion and revenue drivers**
- **AI at LinkedIn:** LinkedIn says Premium revenue reached $1.7B in March 2024, "fueled by AI features" ([LinkedIn News](https://news.linkedin.com/2024/march/LinkedIn-Premium-subscription-tops-1-7B-in-revenue-fueled-by-ai-features)). By Jan 2025 it passed $2B, and nearly 40% of subscribers had used AI features to improve their profiles ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/linkedin-passes-2b-in-premium-revenues-in-12-months-with-overall-revenues-up-9-on-the-year)).
- **AI at Snap:** Lens+ grew "nearly 600% since January 2026", driven by AI Photoshoot. Snapchat+ remains the largest source of subscriber growth ([Snap Newsroom, 2026-11-04](https://newsroom.snap.com/snapchat-subscription-offerings-surpass-30-million)).
- **AI as a reason to raise prices:** X nearly doubled Premium+ to $40 hours after Grok 3 launched ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/18/x-doubles-its-premium-plan-prices-after-xai-releases-grok-3)). Slack raised Business+ from $12.50 to $15 while bundling AI search, recaps, translation and file summaries ([Slack blog](https://slack.com/blog/news/june-2025-pricing-and-packaging-announcement); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/108366-salesforce-latest-price-increase-comes-promise-more-ai.html)).
- **History limits as a lever:** On 2022-09-01 Slack replaced the free plan's 10,000-message and 5 GB cap with a rolling 90-day history window ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/18/slack-is-increasing-prices-and-changing-the-way-its-free-plan-works/); [TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2022/07/27/examining-slacks-new-free-plan-restrictions-and-motivations/)). From 2024-08-26, Slack began deleting free-workspace content older than one year ([TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2024/06/24/slack-to-stop-storing-historical-content-for-free-workspaces/)). Zulip, a competitor, argued the change hurt low-volume teams most and caused some teams to leave ([Zulip blog](https://blog.zulip.com/2022/08/26/why-slacks-free-plan-change-is-causing-an-exodus/)).
- **Storage limits as a lever:** In Sept 2025 Snapchat capped free Memories at 5 GB, with paid tiers of 100 GB for $1.99, 250 GB inside Snapchat+ and 5 TB inside Platinum ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/snapchat-caps-free-memory-storage-launches-paid-storage-plans)).
- **Benchmarks: RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026** (published March 2026, 2025 data, 115,000+ apps), as summarized by [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/) (primary: [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)):
  - Day-35 download-to-paid conversion: 10.7% for hard paywalls vs 2.1% for freemium. One-year retention is almost the same for both (annual plans 27–28%).
  - First-year value per payer: AI apps $30.16 vs non-AI $21.37 (+41%). AI apps retain worse: 21.1% vs 30.7% annual retention.
  - By region: median first-year value per payer is $23 globally, $32 in North America, and **$14 in India and Southeast Asia**. Download-to-paid conversion is 2.6% in North America vs **1.4% in India and Southeast Asia**.
  - Trials of 17 days or more convert at 42.5% vs 25.5% for shorter trials.
- RevenueCat's 2025 edition, per secondary coverage: hard paywall 12.1% vs freemium 2.2% median conversion; high-priced apps converted at 9.8% vs 4.3% for low-priced apps ([RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025); [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/hard-paywall-vs-freemium-2026)).

### Inferences
- In 2025–2026, AI is both the most common new paid feature and the most common justification for a price increase (X, Slack, LinkedIn). RevenueCat's data suggests AI raises revenue per payer but also churn.
- History, retention and storage limits work as conversion levers for group or team products (Slack, Polly, Snapchat Memories), but they reliably cause backlash when a free allowance is taken away from existing users.
- India-market economics are much weaker per user: about 44% of North America's first-year value per payer and about half its conversion. This matches the deep India discounts at Snap, Telegram and X.
- B2B tools tie paid features to the admin or buyer role (moderation, branding, analytics, SSO, export). Consumer tools tie them to individual status and convenience.

### Gaps
- No vendor-published data was found ranking *which individual feature* drives Nitro, Telegram Premium or Reddit Premium conversions.
- RevenueCat figures come from a secondary summary (PPC Land). The full report was not read directly.
- No India-specific B2B SaaS conversion benchmark (for example, Slack India free-to-paid) was found.
- No published conversion data was found for "AI rewriting" or tone-assist features specifically.

## 4. Monetizing anonymity or safety, and backlash when apps paywalled safety

### Takeaway
Putting **safety or identity protection behind a paywall has repeatedly caused public backlash**: Twitter's SMS 2FA in 2023, Meta Verified's paid impersonation protection in 2023, and Snapchat Memories storage in 2025. **Charging to reveal or weaken anonymity led to regulatory action**: NGL's $9.99/mo sender "hints" ended in a $5M FTC and LA DA settlement in 2024. **Breaking anonymity promises cost trust** even without a paywall (Glassdoor, 2024). The products that do charge for anonymity or privacy features keep basic anonymity free and charge for convenience or admin control (Polly Pro, LinkedIn private browsing that still shows viewers, Telegram's anonymous numbers, Blind's paid chats).

### Cited Findings
- **Twitter SMS 2FA paywall (Feb 2023):** Twitter said that from March 20 2023 only Twitter Blue subscribers could use SMS two-factor authentication, and non-paying accounts would have it switched off. Security experts objected that it put a security feature behind a paywall; non-subscribers could still use authenticator apps or security keys ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/02/18/1158134620/twitter-blue-two-factor-authentication-2fa); [SecurityWeek](https://securityweek.com/twitter-shuts-off-text-based-2fa-for-non-subscribers/); [Dark Reading](https://darkreading.com/remote-workforce/analysts-slams-twitter-decision-to-disable-sms-based-2fa)). Sources disagree on the exact cutoff date (19, 20 or 30 March).
- **Meta Verified (2023):** Critics argued that identity verification and impersonation protection, which they see as basic platform safety, were being sold. One safety expert called it a "digital caste system", and a UK commentator compared it to a "protection racket". Meta declined to share revenue metrics ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/04/07/creators-upset-meta-twitter-selling-verified-user-badges/); [Time](https://time.com/6257711/facebook-instagram-twitter-paid-verification); [Clean Up The Internet](https://www.cleanuptheinternet.org.uk/amp/meta-s-meta-verifed-pilot-some-initial-analysis)). Sex workers and trans creators warned that the legal-name requirement could expose them ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/10/meta-verified-reveals-legal-name-sex-workers-warn)). Meta Verified for WhatsApp Business is still sold in India today at ₹639 and ₹1,399 on iOS ([App Store IN](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/id1386412985)).
- **NGL (anonymous Q&A app, 2024):** The FTC and the Los Angeles DA alleged that NGL sent fake anonymous messages to trick users into its $9.99/mo NGL Pro, which sold "hints" about who sent a message (phone model, approximate location) and did not reveal identities. The settlement was $4.5M in consumer redress plus a $500K civil penalty, and NGL was banned from serving users under 18 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/ftc-bans-ngl-from-offering-its-anonymous-social-app-to-minors); [FTC](https://search.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/07/anonymous-messaging-app-targeting-teens-read-disturbing-allegations-ftc-los-angeles-da-action); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/ftc-bans-anonymous-messaging-app-ngl-for-minors-rcna160897)).
- **Glassdoor and Fishbowl (Mar 2024):** After buying Fishbowl, which requires verified identities, Glassdoor began requiring all users to be verified and added real names to profiles without consent, taken from email and other data. Users who had posted negative employer reviews reported this, and some deleted their accounts. Glassdoor said anonymity "will still be protected" ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/03/21/glassdoor-180-users-real-names-accounts-employers-trashed-them/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/glassdoor-reportedly-attaches-real-names-to-anonymous-accounts-120058183.html); [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/employment-law-compliance/glassdoor-privacy-concerns)).
- **Snapchat Memories (Sept–Oct 2025):** Capping free storage at 5 GB and charging for more led to one-star reviews and users exporting their Memories instead of paying. Snap said "it's never easy to transition from receiving a service for free to paying for it" and gave a 12-month grace period ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/snapchat-caps-free-memory-storage-launches-paid-storage-plans); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91416690/snapchat-subscription-social-media-gen-z-backlash); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/snapchat-introduces-paid-plans-for-memories-storage-faces-backlash-1.500293058)).
- **Anonymity as a paid feature in a team tool:** Polly's anonymity and privacy options are only in Pro ($29/mo, billed annually) and above. The free and Basic tiers lack them ([Polly pricing](https://www.polly.ai/pricing)).
- **Private browsing as a paid perk:** On LinkedIn, free users who switch to private mode lose "Who's viewed your profile" and their viewer history ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/billing/answer/47992)). Premium members can browse privately and still see their viewers ([The Windows Club](https://www.thewindowsclub.com/private-mode-in-linkedin)). Premium does not reveal people who are browsing privately ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/billing/answer/4508)).
- **Telegram anonymous numbers:** Telegram sells "+888" numbers through Fragment (paid in TON crypto) that let people register without a SIM card. At the 2022 launch they cost about 9–11 TON (about $20) up to 31,500 TON; sales are now on the secondary market only ([ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/telegram-launches-sale-of-anonymous-numbers-on-fragment/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/learn/ecosystems/what-is-fragment-and-how-can-you-buy-telegram-usernames-on-the-ton-blockchain-304420)). Telegram Premium also includes privacy controls such as voice-message privacy settings ([Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq_premium)).
- **Blind:** Anonymous verified-employee posting is free. Money comes from ad removal (Clean Feed), currency for extra DMs and premium chat groups, and a separate employer product ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534965); [TeamBlind forum](https://www.teamblind.com/post/does-blind-charge-qcdky3vk); [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/employer-reviews-platform/vendor/teamblind/product/blind-for-business)).
- **Enterprise anonymous reporting:** AllVoices, Spot and Vault charge the *organization* (about $2,500–$16,000+ a year), never the reporter ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/allvoices); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/hr/spot-profile/); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/205094/Vault-Platform/)).

### Inferences
- Backlash happens when (a) a safety control that used to be free is taken away from non-payers (Twitter SMS 2FA, Snapchat storage), (b) payment buys a way to unmask or partly identify an anonymous user (NGL), or (c) a platform changes how anonymous identities are handled without consent (Glassdoor). The pattern that hasn't drawn notable backlash in these sources: anonymity is free for the person speaking, and the organization or admin pays for oversight tools (AllVoices, Vault, Spot, Polly's anonymity toggle sold to the poll creator).
- Any paid feature that gives a controller or admin *more* insight into anonymous authors is in the same risk category as NGL's "hints" and Glassdoor's name changes. Paid features that give admins workflow, analytics or moderation tools, without changing what is revealed, match the accepted B2B pattern.
- Meta Verified and X Premium show that "verification and impersonation protection" can be sold, but these products attract lasting criticism from safety advocates and press.

### Gaps
- Final court approval of the NGL settlement was not confirmed in the sources reviewed.
- Whether X ever made SMS 2FA free again was not confirmed.
- No sources found on backlash specifically about paywalled *workplace* anonymity (for example, Polly's anonymity in Pro); absence of reports isn't evidence of absence.
- No India-specific press found on backlash to paywalled safety features.
