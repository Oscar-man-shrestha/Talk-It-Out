# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Talk It Out helps teams say difficult things safely: group messages can be sent anonymously, an AI rewrites them in a calm tone, and a group controller reviews them before anyone else sees them. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

## What we collect

**Account.** Your name and email address. Your password is stored only as a secure hash (Argon2). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and Google account ID.

**Profile (optional).** Headline, bio, location, links, skills, experience, education, profile photo, cover photo and a public @handle if you choose one.

**Group messages.** What you write in a group, any photo you attach, whether you sent it anonymously, and the AI rewrite of it. Photos are stripped of location, camera and author details when uploaded.

**Questions and comments.** Questions, comments, likes and reports you make in the public feed.

**Feed personalisation.** The interests you pick, and signals such as which questions you open or mark "Not interested". You can turn personalisation off in Profile; then this activity is not recorded.

**Safety.** People you block and questions you report.

**On your device only.** Saved questions, recent people in Add Members and your sign-in tokens stay on your device and are never sent to us.

We do not collect your contacts, location, microphone or camera (other than a photo you choose to attach), and we do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Who can see what

- **Anonymous group messages:** other members see "Anonymous user". The group's controller sees who sent it, because the controller reviews every message. This is shown next to the Anonymous checkbox before you send.
- **Your original wording:** only you and the group's controller. Other members see only the approved rewrite.
- **Questions, comments and your name on them:** every signed-in user. The public feed is never anonymous.
- **Your profile:** signed-in users, except people you blocked or who blocked you. Your email is shown only to you.
- **Who reported or reacted:** nobody. Only counts are shown.

## How we use it

To run the service: deliver and review messages, rewrite their tone, rank and search questions, send password-reset emails and keep people safe (blocking, reporting). The AI that rewrites messages and the language features (topic suggestions, search, "Already asked?", tone hints) run on our own servers or on your device; we do not send your messages to third-party AI companies. We never sell your data.

## How long we keep it

- Account and profile: until you delete your account.
- Feed signals (opens, saves, "Not interested"): 180 days.
- Messages and questions: until they are deleted by you, the group's controller or account deletion.

## Deleting your data

Open **Profile → Delete account** and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photos, questions, comments, likes, interests, feed signals, blocks, reports and unpublished messages. Group messages that were already published stay in their groups so conversations still make sense, but they are changed to "Anonymous user" and are no longer linked to you. Groups you controlled are handed to another member. You can also ask us to delete your account by email (below).

## Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, download a copy or ask questions by emailing us, withdraw consent by deleting your account, and complain to your local data protection authority. In India, you can contact our Grievance Officer at the address below; we reply within 30 days.

## Children

Talk It Out is for people aged 18 and over.

## Security

Connections are encrypted with HTTPS in production, passwords are hashed, sessions can be ended on every device from Profile, and photos are cleaned of hidden details. No system is perfectly secure; we will tell affected users about a breach as the law requires.

## Changes

We will show a notice in the app before material changes take effect.
