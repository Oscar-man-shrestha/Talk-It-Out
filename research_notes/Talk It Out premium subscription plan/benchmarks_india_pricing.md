# Subscription benchmarks and India pricing for a freemium social/productivity app (2025–2026)

Scope note: the main datasets are RevenueCat *State of Subscription Apps 2026* (115,000+ apps, $16B+ revenue, published March 2026) and Adapty *State of In-App Subscriptions 2026* (16,000+ apps, $3B revenue, 2025 data, published March 2026). RevenueCat does not report India on its own. It uses an **"IN/SEA" region (India + Southeast Asia)**, so every "India" app-store benchmark below is really IN/SEA. Its web page also contradicts itself in several places, and those are flagged inline. Accessed Nov 2026.

## 1. Freemium-to-paid, trial-to-paid and install-to-paid conversion by category

### Takeaway
Globally, a freemium app converts about **2.1% of downloads to paid within 35 days**, against about 10.7% for a hard paywall (RevenueCat 2026). In IN/SEA the median download-to-paid is only **about 0.7%**, and trial-to-paid is **15.2%** against 34.2% in North America. Business apps convert best of the categories relevant here. Social & Lifestyle and Productivity conversion figures were not visible in the public pages.

### Cited Findings
**Global, RevenueCat 2026 (definitions: download-to-trial is measured at D30; download-to-paid is the share of installs with at least one paid subscription within 35 days)**
- Overall median download-to-paid (D35) is **2.0%**. It is **10.7% for hard paywalls** (top quartile >20.0%) and **2.1% for freemium** (top quartile >4.5%). Hard-paywall conversion fell from 12.1% in the 2025 edition, while freemium held at 2.1% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/); [RevenueCat blog: 2026 trends](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- The top 10% of hard-paywall apps reach 38.7% conversion — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 (via search summary)](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Revenue per install at D60 is **$3.09 for hard paywalls vs $0.38 for freemium** (8x) — [RevenueCat blog: 2026 trends](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- Download-to-trial (D30) medians by category: **Business 9.1%** (highest; top quartile >16.2%), Health & Fitness 6.9%, Education 6.5%, Utilities 6.5%, Gaming 4.4%, Travel 4.1%, Media & Entertainment 4.0%. By price point: high 8.9%, mid 5.4%, low 4.4% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Business](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-business); [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Download-to-paid (D35) medians by category: Health & Fitness 2.9%, **Business 2.6%** (top quartile >5.0%), Shopping 1.3%, Gaming 1.0% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Business](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-business)
- Trial-to-paid medians by category: Travel 43.5%, Health & Fitness 37.7%, Gaming 25.0%, Photo & Video 22.2%. Social, Productivity and Business were not in the text I could read — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Trial-to-paid by store: 32.6% on the App Store vs 32.5% on Google Play. Download-to-paid D35 is 2.6% on iOS vs 0.9% on Google Play in one section, but the summary says 2.9% vs 2.6%, so the source conflicts — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- AI apps have a higher trial start rate (8.5% vs 5.6%) and download-to-paid (2.4% vs 2.0%), but churn faster — [ppc.land on RevenueCat 2026](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/)
- **Productivity:** 71.9% of Productivity conversions happen on Day 0 (50.6% overall) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Productivity](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-productivity)

**Global, Adapty 2026**
- Install-to-trial is 14.5% in North America vs 7.6–10.2% in other regions — [Adapty: free trial vs direct purchase](https://adapty.io/blog/free-trial-vs-direct-purchase-subscription-apps/)
- At upper-mid pricing, install-to-trial is 9.8% for weekly plans, 1.8% for annual and 0.3% for monthly — [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)
- A search summary reported a 27.8% install-to-trial and a "59.2% trial-to-paid" for Adapty. Adapty's own blog uses **59.2% as the first-renewal rate of weekly-plan trial users** (vs 37.0% for direct buyers), so the "trial-to-paid" label looks like a conflation. Do not cite 59.2% as trial-to-paid — [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/); [Adapty report page](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-2026)

**India / IN/SEA, RevenueCat 2026**
- Download-to-trial is 3.0–3.7% for IN/SEA, LATAM, MEA and ROW, vs **North America 7.1%**, Asia-Pacific 5.7% and Western Europe 5.0% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Trial-to-paid is **15.2% in IN/SEA** vs North America 34.2%, APAC 31.9%, Western Europe 29.7%, and about 20–23% for LATAM, MEA and ROW — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Download-to-paid D35 is **0.7% in IN/SEA** in one section and **1.4%** in another (source conflict), vs North America 2.8% (also given as 2.6% and 2.56%), APAC 2.4%, Western Europe 2.0% and LATAM 1.5% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Revenue per install: **IN/SEA $0.08 at D14 and $0.11 at D60**, vs North America $0.38 and $0.55. IN/SEA D60 RPI is **$0.15 on iOS vs $0.04 on Android** — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)

**India market level, Sensor Tower via press**
- India generates about **$0.03 in revenue per download**, vs more than $0.20 in Southeast Asia and Latin America, and was not among the top 20 markets for consumer app spending in 2025 — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/22/indias-app-market-is-booming-but-global-platforms-are-capturing-most-of-the-gains/)
- India's in-app purchase revenue grew from $520M (2021) to **more than $1B in 2025**, with $1.25B projected for 2026 — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/22/indias-app-market-is-booming-but-global-platforms-are-capturing-most-of-the-gains/); [Business Standard, Apr 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/india-app-downloads-revenue-gap-sensor-tower-report-126042400618_1.html)
- Q2 2026 consumer spending in India hit a record **$345M, up 35% YoY**, the fastest growth among major markets, while the US fell 3%. Non-gaming was 68% of India's app revenue in H1 2026, up from 58% three years earlier. India's revenue per download has more than doubled in 3.5 years but is still a "small fraction" of the US figure (about $4.60) — [TechCrunch, Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/31/india-is-starting-to-pay-for-apps-not-just-download-them/)
- India app downloads were 25.5B in 2025 — [TechCrunch, Jan 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/indias-app-downloads-rebounded-to-25-5-billion-in-2025-fueled-by-ai-assistants-and-microdrama-boom/)
- Google One was India's highest-grossing app in Q2 2026. ChatGPT and Claude together took about 83% of India's AI-app revenue — [TechCrunch, Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/31/india-is-starting-to-pay-for-apps-not-just-download-them/)

**Revenue concentration (useful for setting realistic solo-founder expectations)**
- The top 10% of apps capture 94.5% of subscription revenue. Median monthly revenue for new apps fell 22% ($627 to $492). 57.7% of new apps earn $1–$1,000 — [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)
- In their first two years, 14.7% of new Business apps reach $1K monthly revenue and 1.6% reach $10K. The median time to $1K MRR is 113 days — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Business](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-business)
- iOS takes 84.75% of subscription revenue vs Android 15.25%, despite Android having about 70% of global users — [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)

### Inferences
- A freemium app launched mainly to Indian users, where Android is the main platform, should plan around **0.5–1.0% install-to-paid within ~35 days**, not the global 2.1%. Even the 2.1% freemium global median is a median across all categories and regions, and IN/SEA conversion is a third to a half of North America's at every funnel step.
- The best-fit "category" for a group/collaboration tool is probably RevenueCat's **Business** category (download-to-trial 9.1%, D35 2.6%), with Social & Lifestyle as the downside case. The Business numbers come from apps that are mostly paywall-first, though.
- IN/SEA monetization skews toward iOS ($0.15 vs $0.04 D60 RPI). Indian iOS users are worth about 4x Android users per install.

### Gaps
- No India-only (as opposed to IN/SEA) conversion figures were found in RevenueCat or Adapty public pages. Adapty's report reportedly has a country filter behind an email gate.
- Trial-to-paid and download-to-paid for **Social & Lifestyle** and **Productivity** were not in the readable portions of the RevenueCat pages. The full PDF (about 330 pages) likely has them.
- Sensor Tower and Appfigures do not publish install-to-paid rates for India by category.

## 2. Trial length, opt-in vs opt-out trials, hard vs soft paywalls, paywall placement

### Takeaway
Longer trials convert better (**17–32 days: 42.5% vs ≤4 days: 25.5%**), yet apps are moving toward short trials (46.5% now use ≤4 days). Most trials start on **Day 0**, which shows onboarding paywalls do most of the work. Card-required (opt-out) trials convert roughly 2–3x better than no-card trials in SaaS. App-store trials are opt-out by design.

### Cited Findings
- Trial-to-paid by trial length: **≤4 days 25.5%, 5–9 days 37.4%, 10–16 days 35.4%, 17–32 days 42.5%** (RevenueCat buckets, not 3/7/14/30) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Share of apps by trial length: ≤4 days **46.5%** (up from 42.1% in 2025), 5–9 days 39.9% (down from 43.5%), 10+ days about 14–15%, 17–32 days 5.0% (down from 6.1%) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/); [RevenueCat blog: 2026 trends](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- **55.4% of 3-day-trial cancellations happen on Day 0** (up from about 51% in 2025), and 84% happen on Day 0–1 — [RevenueCat blog: 2026 trends](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- Share of trial starts on Day 0: **Business 89.9%** (highest), Health & Fitness 82.1%, Gaming 81.5%, Education 78.5%, **Productivity 78%** (lowest, with a 22% "delayed trial" rate) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/); [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Productivity](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-productivity)
- Adapty: about 90% of trial starts are on install day (89.4% globally). About 7 in 10 apps offer trials, a share unchanged for two years. Days 1–3 account for 2.0% of trial starts and Day 31+ for 6.3% — [Adapty: free trial vs direct purchase](https://adapty.io/blog/free-trial-vs-direct-purchase-subscription-apps/); [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)
- Trial users renew 8–60% better at first renewal than direct buyers, depending on plan. Weekly plans with a trial keep 19% at 12 months vs 13% without one — [Adapty: free trial vs direct purchase](https://adapty.io/blog/free-trial-vs-direct-purchase-subscription-apps/)
- **Productivity is an exception:** direct-purchase 12-month LTV of $56.95 beats trial LTV of $49.13 (+16%). Lifestyle direct buyers are also 21% higher than trial users. Utilities and Education do better with trials — [Adapty: free trial vs direct purchase](https://adapty.io/blog/free-trial-vs-direct-purchase-subscription-apps/)
- Hard paywall vs freemium: D35 conversion 10.7% vs 2.1%, but one-year retention of yearly subscribers is **27% (hard) vs 28% (freemium)**, which the source calls "statistically negligible". Refund rates are 2.5% vs 2.9% — [RevenueCat blog: 2026 trends](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md); [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Experiments on trial structure have the highest win rate (59.6%), vs price (45.5%) and visual/text (34.6%) — [Adapty: free trial vs direct purchase](https://adapty.io/blog/free-trial-vs-direct-purchase-subscription-apps/)
- **Opt-out (card-required) vs opt-in (no card), mostly B2B SaaS, vendor data:** First Page Sage reports 48.8% vs 18.2% ([Userpilot](https://userpilot.com/blog/how-does-a-free-trial/); [CrazyEgg](https://www.crazyegg.com/blog/free-to-paid-conversion-rate/?version=published)). ChartMogul 2026 (via Userpilot) reports card-required trials at 25–35% median free-to-paid vs 4–6% for no-card ([Userpilot](https://userpilot.com/blog/how-to-increase-free-trial-conversion/)). Visionary Marketing 2026 (self-reported, 84,200 trials) reports 60.4% vs 25.2%, with opt-out signups about 30% of opt-in volume ([Visionary Marketing](https://visionary-marketing.co.uk/blog/saas-free-trial-conversion-statistics-2026)). Sixteen Ventures puts best-in-class opt-out above 60% and best opt-in above 25% ([Sixteen Ventures](https://sixteenventures.com/saas-free-trial-benchmarks/)). Definitions differ across these sources.
- Opinion (third-party blog): 7–14-day trials may outperform 3-day trials in India. The blog attributes this loosely to Phiture and ASO firms without data — [appsops.store](https://appsops.store/blog/app-store-pricing-south-asia-2026)

### Inferences
- App Store and Google Play free trials auto-convert unless the user cancels, so they are opt-out by construction. The RevenueCat trial-to-paid figures (about 25–43%) are opt-out benchmarks. A "reverse trial" or no-card in-app trial should be benchmarked against the much lower opt-in SaaS figures.
- For a collaboration tool, value usually appears only after other people join (a group exists, a message gets reviewed). That argues for a **7–14-day trial** over a 3-day one, and for placing a soft paywall at a feature gate (for example a group-size or controller-feature limit) as well as in onboarding. Given the 78% Day-0 trial share for Productivity, a delayed, value-triggered trial offer matters more there than in other categories.
- Since hard-paywall retention matches freemium, a hard paywall is not a retention risk. For a social app that needs network growth, though, a hard paywall would suppress the free users who invite others. Freemium plus a team trial looks like the more consistent fit.

### Gaps
- No public data splits trial length by IN/SEA, and none covers Social or Business categories specifically.
- No app-store dataset was found comparing onboarding-placed and feature-gate-placed paywalls directly. Day-0 trial-start shares are the best proxy.

## 3. Monthly vs annual mix, weekly plans, price points, annual discounts, lifetime

### Takeaway
Median global prices are about **$8–10/month, $34.80/year and $5.99/week** (RevenueCat 2026). The North American median annual price is about 4x the monthly price, roughly a 67% discount versus paying monthly. Weekly plans dominate revenue (55.5% per Adapty) but retain terribly and fit poorly with a productivity or team tool. Productivity skews toward annual plans.

### Cited Findings
- Overall plan mix: 42% monthly, 34% yearly. Elsewhere the same report says each plan type is "roughly a third", so the source is inconsistent — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Category mix: Gaming is 82% weekly. Productivity is described as "77% yearly" in one sentence and as the "monthly outlier at 77%" in another, and "Productivity drives 91% of revenue from monthly plans" also appears. The page conflicts with itself, so verify in the PDF — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Productivity](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-productivity)
- Regional mix: North America is 36% monthly / 40% yearly. MEA is about 52–55% monthly. Latin America is 29% weekly — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Median prices: weekly **$5.99** (most common $5); monthly **$8** in one section and **$10** in another; yearly **$34.80** (up from $31.60; most common $30). Business has the highest weekly median at $6.89. Category monthly medians range from $4.99 to $9.99 — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/); [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Business](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-business)
- Regional medians: North America $9.99/month and $39.99/year. Western Europe yearly $39.44. **IN/SEA $3.75/month and $18.32/year** (about half of North America). On Google Play specifically, IN/SEA medians are **$3.12/month and $14.64/year**, the lowest of any region — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Adapty revenue mix: weekly plans rose from 43.3% of revenue (2023) to 55.5% (2025). Monthly fell from 21.1% to 11.7% and annual from 29.2% to 22.5% — [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)
- Adapty: the best 1-year LTV comes from a weekly $5.99 plan with a 3-day trial (1.5x average). The worst comes from an annual $79.99 plan with no trial — [Adapty: free trial vs direct purchase](https://adapty.io/blog/free-trial-vs-direct-purchase-subscription-apps/)
- Adapty reports European apps charging 29–39% more than North American apps; the monthly gap grew from 6% (2023) to 39% (2025), $15.25 vs $10.95 — [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)
- Lifetime / one-time purchases: in Lifestyle, one-time purchases rose from 5.9% (2023) to 26.3% (2025) of revenue — [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)
- Adapty's country pricing index (as reported in a search summary of its 2026 report): **India 0.6x the US baseline**, Turkey 0.7x, and UK/France/Germany/Italy/Spain 1.2x — [Adapty State of In-App Subscriptions 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-2026) (not verified on the page itself)

### Inferences
- The North America median ($39.99/year vs $9.99/month) implies the yearly plan costs about 4 months of the monthly price. The IN/SEA median ($18.32 vs $3.75) implies about 4.9 months. These are my calculations from the medians, not stated discount figures.
- A Plus tier anchored on "annual ≈ 4–5x monthly", shown as the default selected option, matches both global and IN/SEA practice.
- Weekly plans are a games/utility/AI pattern (Gaming 82% weekly). For a group/productivity tool, monthly plus annual is the more credible pair, and weekly would likely churn fastest (see section 5).

### Gaps
- No source found gives a stated median **annual discount %**. It is only derivable from medians.
- No reliable data was found on lifetime-purchase share for Productivity, Social or Business apps, or in India.
- The Adapty India 0.6x index was not visible in the fetched page text.

## 4. Regional pricing for India: store price tiers, INR price points, payments, and India vs US

### Takeaway
Both stores allow low INR prices: Apple reportedly from about ₹9 since its December 2022 overhaul, and Google Play historically from ₹10. Auto-renewing payments up to **₹15,000 per charge** need no fresh OTP under RBI's e-mandate rules, and Google Play has supported **UPI Autopay** for subscriptions since November 2022. Consumer anchors in India sit at **₹89–₹399/month**. IN/SEA app-subscription medians are about **$3.12–$3.75/month**, while India's revenue per download is a tiny fraction of the US figure.

### Cited Findings
**Store price tiers**
- Apple's December 2022 pricing overhaul (700 new price points, 900 in total) reportedly set an India range of **₹9 up to ₹9,99,900**, in ₹5 steps within the ₹9–499 band. Developers can also pick a base storefront for automatic price equalization — [YourStory, Dec 2022](https://yourstory.com/2022/12/apple-updates-app-store-pricing-system-globally-including-india); [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/apple-changes-its-app-store-pricing-structure-worldwide/story); [Business Standard, Dec 2022](https://business-standard.com/article/technology/apple-overhauls-store-pricing-to-offer-flexibility-for-app-developers-122120701208_1.html) (figures came from a search summary; the YourStory page returned 403, so verify in App Store Connect)
- Apple's India **price-increase consent thresholds** for auto-renewable subscriptions are ₹500 (non-annual) and ₹5,000 (annual). These are consent thresholds, not minimum prices — [Apple App Store Connect Help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/in-app-purchases-and-subscriptions/auto-renewable-subscription-price-increase-thresholds/)
- Google Play lowered India's minimum price for apps and in-app products from ₹50 to **₹10** (2015, one-time purchases) — [Android Developers Blog, 2015](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2015/11/minimum-purchase-price-for-apps-and-in.html); [Google India Blog, 2015](https://india.googleblog.com/2015/09/your-favourite-apps-and-games-with.html)
- 2021: after RBI's recurring-payment rules took effect, Google suspended new auto-renewing subscriptions in India from May 4, 2021, resumed them after September 30, 2021, and **capped India auto-renewing subscriptions below ₹5,000**. This predates RBI's 2022 increase to ₹15,000, so the cap may be outdated — [RevenueCat blog, 2021](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/engineering/indias-new-subscription-rules)
- A third-party blog (opinion, unsourced) says that if no India price is set, Apple's auto-conversion from USD gives a price "structurally too high" for India. It also says **18% GST** applies to digital services and is collected by the store, with proceeds calculated on the pre-tax base — [appsops.store, 2026](https://appsops.store/blog/app-store-pricing-south-asia-2026)

**RBI e-mandates and UPI Autopay**
- RBI raised the extra-authentication-free (no OTP) limit for recurring e-mandates **from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 per transaction** in June 2022 — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/rbi-increases-e-mandate-limit-for-recurring-payments-to-inr-15000); [Business Standard, Jun 2022](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/finance/new-e-mandate-guidelines-rbi-enhances-limit-for-e-mandates-on-credit-debit-cards-to-rs-15-000-122060800417_1.html); [NPCI RuPay circular](https://www.npci.org.in/PDF/npci/rupay/2022/Circular-RuPay-AutoPay-AFA-Limit-enhancement-and-compliance.pdf)
- In April 2026 RBI issued a **consolidated e-mandate framework** covering UPI, cards and prepaid instruments. It keeps ₹15,000 per transaction without OTP, sets ₹1 lakh for mutual funds, insurance and credit-card bill payments, and lets users edit, pause or cancel mandates at any time and set maximum limits for variable mandates — [Business Today, Apr 21 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/rbi-caps-recurring-payments-at-rs15000-without-otp-under-new-e-mandate-framework-526759-2026-04-21); [Business Today, Apr 22 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/rbi-auto-pay-rules-changed-what-happens-to-your-sips-otts-and-other-payments-526829-2026-04-22); [RBI notification](https://website.rbi.org.in/web/rbi/-/notifications/processing-of-e-mandates-for-recurring-transactions-12341)
- Google Play added **UPI Autopay for subscriptions in India in November 2022**. UPI had been accepted for one-time Play purchases since 2019. Users approve the first charge in their UPI app, and later renewals are debited automatically — [Google India Blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/now-pay-for-subscriptions-via-upi-on-google-play/); [Business Standard, Nov 2022](https://business-standard.com/article/finance/google-play-allows-upi-autopay-payment-for-subscription-based-purchases-122111501224_1.html)
- Sensor Tower's analyst credits UPI with reducing friction for in-app purchases in India, without giving figures — [TechCrunch, Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/31/india-is-starting-to-pay-for-apps-not-just-download-them/)
- **Billing failures:** on Google Play, about 31–32% of cancellations are billing failures (28.2% in 2025), vs about 14–15% on the App Store. RevenueCat estimates Android apps can recover 15–20% of lost revenue with better retries and grace periods — [RevenueCat blog: 2026 trends](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md); [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)

**Common INR consumer subscription anchors**
- ChatGPT Go launched in India at **₹399/month** on Aug 19, 2025, payable via UPI — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/openai-launches-cheapest-chatgpt-plan-at-inr-399-in-india/); [Analytics Vidhya](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2025/08/chatgpt-go/)
- Spotify Premium Individual is **₹139/month** (up from ₹119) and Duo is ₹179 (from ₹149); the article is undated — [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/apps/spotify-premium-prices-go-up-in-india-here-are-the-updated-rates.html)

### Inferences
- At an assumed ₹85–90 per USD (not sourced here), the IN/SEA medians of $3.12–$3.75/month equal roughly **₹270–₹335/month**, and $14.64–$18.32/year equals roughly **₹1,250–₹1,650/year**. Those medians include Southeast Asia and high-ARPU categories. A social/productivity Plus tier for Indian individuals sits more credibly at **₹99–₹199/month and ₹799–₹1,499/year**, below ChatGPT Go (₹399) and around Spotify (₹139) and YouTube Lite (₹89).
- All plausible prices for this app are far below the ₹15,000 no-OTP limit and the ₹500 Apple consent threshold. RBI rules therefore should not add friction beyond the first UPI or card mandate approval, though pre-debit notices and user-side mandate controls make renewals easy to cancel.
- Because India is Android-heavy and Google Play shows about double the billing-failure share of the App Store, enabling Play's grace period and account hold, and nudging users to UPI Autopay, is probably worth more than fine-tuning price.

## 5. Churn and retention by category and region

### Takeaway
Subscription retention is low and getting worse: **monthly plans keep only about 6–14% of subscribers at Year 1**, and annual first renewal sits at **20–40%**. About 72% of annual subscribers cancel within year 1 in the 2026 data, up from 56%. Social & Lifestyle has the weakest renewal of any category, Business the strongest, and IN/SEA trails North America at every billing cycle.

### Cited Findings
- Monthly plans: median Y1 retention **6–14%** (top quartile 11–25%). Freemium 8% vs hard paywall 9%. Median 6+ month retention is 14–26% (top quartile 30–50%) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Weekly plans: median Y1 retention 1–2%, down from 1.7% (2023 cohort) to 1.2% (2024 cohort). 6+ month retention is 5–15% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Yearly plans: median Y1 retention **20–40%** (top quartile 32–59%), down from 31% to 28% between the 2023 and 2024 cohorts. By price: low 36%, mid 26%, high 23%. Yearly first renewal by category runs 23–40%, from **Productivity lowest at 23%** to **Business highest at 40%** — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- First-renewal rates by billing cycle: weekly 35–58%, monthly 53–61%. **Social & Lifestyle: weekly 35%, monthly 42% (both lowest).** **Business: monthly 61% (highest).** 6+ month retention: Business 26% weekly / 40% monthly, Social & Lifestyle 11% weekly / 24% monthly — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- By region, first renewal: **IN/SEA weekly 37%, monthly 46%, yearly 22%**, vs North America 55% / 55% / 26% and Western Europe 42% / 55% / 28% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Annual plans: Month 1 accounts for **35% of annual cancellations**, with 3–10% per period mid-year and a rise again around Month 12. About **72% of annual subscribers cancel in Year 1 in 2026, vs about 56% in 2025** ("cancel" here means auto-renew turned off) — [RevenueCat blog: 2026 trends](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- Productivity specifics: monthly active renewal 28.2%, yearly active renewal 85.6%, monthly reactivation 36.1% (the highest in the dataset), and 44% of cancellations cite cost — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Refund rates: **IN/SEA 7.7% median (highest region)** vs North America 3.4%. **Productivity 4.7% (highest category)**. By price tier: low 2.7%, mid 3.9%, high 4.5% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Cancellation causes: billing errors are 15.2% of App Store cancellations vs 32.2% on Google Play. Unsubscribes are 82.9% vs 66.3% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- AI monthly plans retain 36% worse over 12 months than traditional apps — [RevenueCat blog: 2026 trends](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- Adapty: Europe's D380 annual retention is 21.3% vs North America 20.0% — [Adapty 2026 key findings](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)

### Inferences
- For planning in IN/SEA: expect about **46% of monthly payers to renew once**, roughly 20–25% to still be paying after 6 months, and only about **22% of annual subscribers to renew** in year 2. Pushing annual plans early (cash up front) matters more in India than in the US.
- A team tier should land near the **Business** retention profile (61% monthly first renewal, 40% yearly) rather than Social & Lifestyle (42% monthly). That gap is a strong retention argument for a seat/team plan over an individual social plan.
- The high IN/SEA refund rate (7.7%) points to clear trial-end reminders and precise paywall copy.

### Gaps
- No Month-1 retention figure for monthly plans was reported separately. First-renewal rate is the closest proxy.
- No India-only (as opposed to IN/SEA) churn data was found, and no Social & Lifestyle × region cross-tab.

## 6. B2B per-seat pricing for small Indian teams and free-tier limits

### Takeaway
Self-serve team chat in India is cheap. **Slack Pro is ₹245.25/user/month billed yearly (₹294.75 monthly)** on its India page, and Zoho Cliq paid tiers are reportedly around **₹90–₹240/user/month**. Notion bills in USD at $10/seat for Plus. The most visible upgrade trigger is Slack Free's **90-day message history and 10-app limit**.

### Cited Findings
- **Slack (India pricing page):** Free is ₹0 with 90 days of message history, up to 10 apps, 1:1 huddles only, 1:1 Slack Connect, and deletion of data after one year. **Pro costs ₹245.25 per user per month billed yearly or ₹294.75 monthly** and adds unlimited history and apps, group huddles, and external channels with up to 250 orgs. **Business+ costs ₹557.10 yearly or ₹668.25 monthly.** Enterprise+ is contact-sales. Slack's fair billing policy charges only active members (active within 28 days) and gives prorated credits for inactive ones — [Slack India pricing](https://slack.com/intl/en-in/pricing)
- Slack's help center lists higher India **list** prices: Pro ₹655/user/month monthly and ₹6,540/year (₹545/month). Business+ is ₹1,125 monthly. A "self-serve discount" applies for teams in India, which likely explains why the pricing page shows ₹245–₹295. The two sources conflict, and the pricing page is the more current customer-facing number — [Slack Help: India-issued cards](https://slack.com/help/articles/5187182032403-Resume-automatic-payments-on-an-India-issued-credit-card); [Slack Pro India](https://slack.com/intl/en-in/pricing/pro). A reseller quotes Pro at ₹245/user/month annual excluding 18% GST — [cloudfysystems](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/slack-pricing-plans-india-2026)
- **Zoho Cliq:** the official comparison lists Free, Standard, Professional and Enterprise tiers. Professional and Enterprise require a **minimum of 10 users**. Storage is 100 GB per organization on Free/Standard and 100 GB per user on higher tiers — [Zoho Cliq plan comparison](https://prezohoweb.zoho.com/cliq/plan-comparison.html). INR prices come only from third parties and conflict: Professional ₹90 and Enterprise ₹180/user/month yearly (₹100 / ₹200 monthly) per one reseller, Standard ₹120 and Enterprise ₹240 per another, and USD $2 / $4 on Capterra — [itforsme](https://itforsme.in/pricing/zoho-cliq-india); [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/167639/zoho-cliq). A 2023 press report put an "Unlimited" plan at ₹36/user/month, likely outdated — [CXOToday](https://www.cxotoday.com/press-release/zoho-ramps-up-its-collaboration-platform-cliq-launches-smart-conference-rooms-solution/)
- **Notion:** Plus is US$10/seat/month and Business US$20, charged per workspace member. No official INR price list was found. A reseller estimates ₹900–₹1,000/member/month annual through its channel. One source says Notion bills in USD from the US entity with no Indian GST invoice — [Notion pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing); [cloudfysystems](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/notion-pricing-india-2026); [itforsme](https://itforsme.in/pricing/notion-india)
- RevenueCat: Business-category apps earn a median Y1 RLTV per payer of **$35.48** (top quartile >$69.19), D14 RPI $0.31 and D60 RPI $0.50 — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Business](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-business)

### Inferences
- A seat price for small Indian teams that undercuts Slack Pro (₹245/user/month annual) and sits near Zoho Cliq (about ₹90–₹240) points to roughly **₹49–₹149 per seat per month**, or a flat small-team bundle. Zoho's 10-user minimum on higher tiers suggests Indian teams tolerate minimum seat counts.
- Slack's free tier gates **history (90 days), integrations (10) and group calls**. The equivalent levers for this app would be group count or size, history depth, and controller/review features. Slack's active-user billing is a fairness norm that Indian SMB buyers may expect.
- App stores do not natively bill "per seat" for a team. A team tier sold in-app would likely be fixed bundles (for example up to 5 or 10 members), with per-seat billing handled on the web. I did not verify this against store policy.
- Anything billed in USD (like Notion) gives Indian buyers no GST invoice. INR billing with GST invoices is a likely selling point for Indian teams, though no source here measures it.

### Gaps
- No survey or press data (Inc42, YourStory, Entrackr) was found on what Indian startups actually pay per seat, or on their seat counts. Only list prices were found.
- Zoho Cliq's official INR prices and free-tier message-history limits could not be confirmed (the en-in pricing page returned 404). Third-party figures conflict.
- Notion's free-plan limits for multi-member workspaces were not checked in this pass.
- Freshworks pricing (Freshchat, Freshdesk) was not researched. It is less comparable to group chat.
- No benchmark was found for team-plan conversion or seat expansion rates for small Indian teams.
