# Delete your Talk It Out account

**In the app:** open **Profile**, scroll to **Delete account**, enter your password and confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

**By email:** if you cannot sign in, email talkitoutapp.mail@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

Your account, profile and photos, questions, comments, likes, interests, feed activity, blocks, reports and messages that were never published.

## What is kept

Group messages that were already published stay in their group so the conversation still makes sense, but they are shown as "Anonymous user" and are no longer linked to you. Groups you controlled are handed to another member. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
